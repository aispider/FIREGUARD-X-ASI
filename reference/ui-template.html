      NASA evidence
      • Oxygen, flow velocity and geometry change flame growth, spread and extinction (BASS-II).
      • Flame growth and extinction depend on flow, oxygen, pressure and heating (SoFIE-GEL).
      • Larger-scale spread tests inform spacecraft fire safety (Saffire).
      Prototype assumptions
      • Term weights: oxygen 42%, airflow 26%, temperature 20%, heating 10%, pressure 8%.
      • Airflow effect peaks near 13 cm/s and falls at high flow (blow-off).
      • Fuel, geometry and gravity multipliers are hand-set.
      Synthetic simulation values
      • Every score on this page is computed in the browser from the inputs.
      • No value is a NASA measurement until the ingestion pipeline supplies it.
